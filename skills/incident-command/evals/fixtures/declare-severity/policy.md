Severity is set by user impact, not by cause. Use the highest level whose criteria match.
- SEV1: checkout or login failing for 25% or more of users; or customers charged incorrectly; or personal data exposed.
- SEV2: checkout or login failing for 5% to under 25% of users; or a major feature down with no workaround.
- SEV3: any other user-visible impact.
Internal updates every 30 min (SEV1), 60 min (SEV2), 4 h (SEV3), counted from the declaration or the last update.
Roles: IC, ops lead, comms lead, scribe.
