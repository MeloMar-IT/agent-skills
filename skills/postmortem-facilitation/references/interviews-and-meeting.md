# Question bank and agenda

Sources: Google SRE book ch. 15 (https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/); Allspaw, "The Infinite Hows" (https://www.kitchensoap.com/2014/11/14/the-infinite-hows-or-the-dangers-of-the-five-whys/).

## Questions (fill in the event)
- Walk me through what you saw at T<n>.
- What were you expecting to happen at T<n>?
- What information did you have at T<n>, and where did it come from?
- Which part of T<n> was hardest to work out?
- What helped you most at T<n>?
- How did you decide what to try next at T<n>?
- Who else did you talk to at T<n>, and what did they tell you?
- Tell me about anything at T<n> that surprised you.

## Agenda (60-90 min)
1. Ground rules and purpose (5 min).
2. Walk the timeline; each person adds what they saw.
3. Contributing conditions.
4. What went well.
5. Actions: owner, due date, success check.
6. Parking lot and recorded disagreements.
